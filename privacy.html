Privacy Policy

Effective November 14, 2026

Dial ("Dial", "we", "us") is an iPhone app that helps people reduce their screen time. This policy explains what information Dial collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. We have tried to keep it short and plain.

The short version: Your screen time data stays on your iPhone. If you use friends and leagues, Dial shares only points, results and milestones that your iPhone works out, never your screen time amounts or which apps you use. We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with data brokers, and you can delete your account at any time.

Screen Time data

Dial uses Apple's Screen Time frameworks (Family Controls, Managed Settings and Device Activity), which you authorize in iOS. Through these frameworks, Apple does not reveal which specific apps or websites you use. Dial sees only opaque references to the apps and categories you choose to manage, and works out your screen time from usage thresholds being reached.

Your screen time history (daily and weekly, overall and by category), the limits you reach and the effect of the reduction tools you use are calculated and kept on your iPhone. Dial's servers do not receive your screen time amounts, your usage history, or the apps and categories you select.

Information we collect

  • Account information: the details you provide to create an account, such as a display name and email address.
  • Points and results: if you join leagues or add friends, the league points, matchup results and milestones that your iPhone works out. These never include screen time amounts or app information.
  • Social and league activity: your friends list, invitations, and the leagues you join or create.
  • Technical information: basic diagnostic and crash information needed to keep the app working.

How we use it

  • To run your account, friends and leagues.
  • To score matchups and standings using the points and results your iPhone sends.
  • To share your milestones and results with friends, if you choose to.
  • To maintain, secure and improve the app.

Your history, trends, projections and the effect of the reduction tools you use are shown to you on your iPhone.

Who can see your information

  • Friends: only the milestones and results you choose to make visible to them.
  • League members: people in a league you join can see your display name, points and matchup results in that league, never your screen time amounts.
  • Service providers: companies that host and operate our servers, only as needed to run Dial and under obligations to protect your data.
  • Legal requirements: where required by law.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your Screen Time data for advertising, profiling or any purpose unrelated to helping you manage your screen time.

Your choices

  • You can revoke Dial's Screen Time access at any time in iOS Settings.
  • You control what your friends can see in the app.
  • You can delete your account, and the information associated with it, from within the app or by contacting us.

Data retention and security

We keep your information while your account is active and delete it when you delete your account, except where we must keep it to meet legal obligations. We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information, but no system is completely secure.

Children

Dial is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the updated version on this page and change the effective date above.
