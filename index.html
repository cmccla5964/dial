Turn down your screen time. For good.

Dial is an iPhone app built to help you use your phone less, and keep it that way. Track your progress over time, see what actually works for you, and compete with friends to stay on track.

Coming soon to iPhone

Screen time leagues

Join a public league or start a private one with friends. Face off in weekly one-on-one matchups over a season, earning points for using your phone less.

Your history, all in one place

See your daily and weekly screen time, overall and by category, and how it changes over time.

Milestones worth chasing

Dial recognizes your personal bests, like your lowest screen time day of the year, so progress feels like progress.

Levers that show their impact

Turn on tools like app limits and grayscale, see how each one is expected to change your screen time, and track the real effect against your own history.

Better with friends

Add friends and share your milestones and progress, only with the people you choose.
